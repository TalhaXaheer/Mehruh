# Design Guardrails

Source of truth: `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`. These are the rules that stop the site looking generic.

**Do**
- Generous whitespace. Sections breathe: 120–200px vertical padding desktop, 72–96px mobile.
- One consistent content grid (12-col, max-width 1280, side padding fluid). Nav, sections, and footer ALL align to it.
- Serif display (Cormorant Garamond) for headlines, wide-tracked small caps sans (Inter) for labels.
- Gold used sparingly: thin rules, small labels, hover reveals, one primary CTA per view.
- Square or 2px corners. Hairline 1px borders at low opacity.
- Alternate section tone for rhythm (see section rhythm in DESIGN_SYSTEM).

**Don't**
- No rounded-xl cards, drop-shadow soup, gradients on gradients, glassmorphism everywhere.
- No emoji, no stock "sparkle" icons as decoration.
- No more than 2 typefaces. No more than 1 accent color (gold).
- No centered-everything. Mix left-aligned editorial layouts with centered moments.
- No filler copy like "Lorem ipsum" or generic marketing phrases ("elevate your experience").
- No visible logo image background boxes. The monogram must be a transparent SVG/PNG.
- No text placed over a busy part of the hero video without the overlay passing contrast.
